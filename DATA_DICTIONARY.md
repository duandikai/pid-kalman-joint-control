# Figure and table data mapping

All paths below are relative to `results/data/`, either generated by `main`
or extracted from the local delivery's `reference_results.zip`. Figure numbers refer to the
revised manuscript structure, not to the numbering in the archived script.
New plots are generated from the reconstructed numerical results; they are
not asserted to be pixel-identical to the manuscript's existing images.

For every trajectory, `*_metrics.csv` reports the following definitions:

- Angle/velocity tracking RMSE and maximum absolute error: reference minus
  true plant state, over 1 <= t <= 5 s.
- Voltage/current RMS: applied voltage and clipped current over that window.
- Encoder/gyro/KF estimation RMSE: measurement or posterior estimate minus
  true plant state. Primary metrics use 1–5 s; `_full_` metrics use 0–5 s.
- Step settling: first sample after the final violation of the 2% band through
  5 s, or NaN if still outside the band at the end. This is a finite-horizon
  observation, not a guarantee that it remains settled indefinitely.
- Step overshoot: max(0,(maximum true angle - 30 deg)/30 deg * 100).

Angles and velocities in trajectory CSVs are in degrees and degrees/second.
Dynamics and the KF operate internally in radians. `voltage_command_V` is
the raw PID output; `applied_voltage_V` is the saturated voltage used by the
motor model. `feedback_used_deg` is the previous sample used by the
controller. `controller_error_deg` includes this timing and differs from
`tracking_error_deg`, which is evaluated against the true current state.

K11 and K22 are dimensionless; K12 has units of seconds and K21 has units of
1/second. P11 is angle variance in rad^2, P12 is angle/velocity covariance in
rad^2/s, and P22 is velocity variance in rad^2/s^2. P11_deg2 is the converted
angle variance in deg^2.

All parameters are exported. The script uses SI units except for explicitly
named `_deg`/`_Hz` parameters. `encoder_std` is in radians and `gyro_std` in
radians/second. Gains multiply angle error in radians to produce volts:
Kp is V/rad, Ki is V/(rad s), and Kd is V s/rad. The manuscript's shorthand
gain units should be clarified accordingly.
